上传什么格式的书/文档？

支持 PDF、EPUB、MOBI、AZW3、TXT 与 Markdown。建议上传有文字层（可选中/复制文字）的文件；纯扫描图片且无文字层的 PDF 暂不支持整本识别。

蒸馏提示「提炼点不足」？

蒸馏一本书会按书的内容大小消耗提炼点（约每 30 万字消耗 1 点，单本 1–10 点）。免费用户每月享有固定提炼点与调用次数，用完后可在 App 内升级 Pro 或购买提炼点/调用加购包。

AI 没有回复 / 回复失败

AI 功能需要联网，通过我们的服务（readact.shxlab.com）调用第三方大语言模型（以 DeepSeek 为主）。请确认：1) 手机网络可用；2) 稍等片刻后重试。若持续失败，请截图页面并邮件反馈给我们。

AI 回复很慢

首次调用需要连接 AI 服务并加载上下文，通常需数秒至十余秒；网络不佳或内容较长时会更慢，请耐心等待，不要重复发送。

为什么生成内容带引用？

为保证可追溯，蒸馏产物与问答/产出内容会附上原书中的引用片段。为尊重版权，单条引用不超过 150 字、单书累计引用比例不超过全书 15%。

我的书与数据存在哪里？

书籍、方法库、问答与偏好默认保存在设备本地。若你开启了 iCloud，数据会通过你的 Apple ID（CloudKit/SwiftData）自动同步到你的其他设备；除 AI 处理所需的必要文本分块外，我们不会上传到自有服务器。

如何删除我的数据？

删除单本书籍：书籍详情页 →「删除书籍」。清除全部数据：设置 →「清除 iCloud 数据」。删除 App 后，可在系统设置中删除来自 iCloud 的数据。

订阅 / 加购问题

订阅与加购通过 App Store 完成。管理或取消订阅请前往「系统设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」。若已购买但未生效，请先尝试在 App 内恢复购买，仍无效则附上订单截图邮件反馈。

Which formats are supported?

We support PDF, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, TXT, and Markdown. Files with a selectable/copyable text layer are recommended; scanned PDFs without a text layer are not yet supported for whole-book recognition.

"Not enough distill points"?

Distilling a book consumes distill points based on its size (about 1 point per 300,000 words, 1–10 points per book). Free users get a fixed monthly allowance of points and calls. When exhausted, upgrade to Pro or buy a distill-points/calls add-on in the App.

AI not responding / request failed

AI features require an internet connection and are routed through our service (readact.shxlab.com) to third-party large language models (primarily DeepSeek). Please make sure: 1) your network is available; 2) wait a moment and retry. If it keeps failing, take a screenshot and email us.

AI replies are slow

The first call needs to connect to the AI service and load context, which usually takes a few to a dozen-plus seconds; a poor network or long content can make it slower. Please be patient and do not send duplicate messages.

Why do generated results include citations?

For traceability, distilled outputs and Q&A/production content include excerpts from the source book. To respect copyright, each citation is limited to 150 characters, and cumulative excerpts per book are limited to 15% of the book.

Where is my data stored?

Books, methodology libraries, Q&A, and preferences are stored locally on your device by default. If you enable iCloud, data is automatically synced to your other devices through your Apple ID (CloudKit/SwiftData). Except for the necessary text chunks required for AI processing, we do not upload your content to our own servers.

How do I delete my data?

To delete a single book: open the book detail page → "Delete Book". To clear all data: Settings → "Clear iCloud Data". After deleting the App, you can also delete data from iCloud in System Settings.

Subscription / Add-on issues

Subscriptions and add-ons are purchased through the App Store. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to "System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions". If a purchase hasn't taken effect, first try restoring purchases in the App; if it still fails, email us with a screenshot of your receipt.
